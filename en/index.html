About
🎬 Rombee

I edit in two lanes —
and both are busy

I'm Roman, an editor working under the name Rombee. For the past three years I've been the sole editor for a four-channel EA FC / FIFA network — pack openings, trading, pitch notes, TOTY and launch-season content, long-form and clips.

In parallel I cut science, health and beauty explainers for a media company across three more channels — whiteboard-led educational shorts, event coverage and interviews.

  • ✅ Revisions included
